Product & UX Design

LA

I design products, interfaces, flows, and the systems that hold them together. Mostly tools people have to use rather than want to use, which is where design either earns its keep or doesn’t. The dates and job titles are further down; they are the least interesting thing on this page.

Also true

  • I cook, and I post it. There is a TikTok account that exists only for this. I invented a plantain pasta, Picante Plantain Pasta, and have run several experiments since. Nobody has been hurt.
  • I grew cucumbers as a child. My family farmed a little and I was part of it. The plan, eventually, is a small cottage in a small village, a small family, and food I grew myself.
  • I write fiction under a name that isn’t this one. That is genuinely all you’re getting. It is the same job as this one though: decide what to cut, hold attention through a sequence, and accept that the sentence in the empty state does more work than the illustration above it.
  • This is one of about four photographs of myself I can tolerate. If we work together, please don’t schedule a headshot day.
